Story Videos Uploader

A personal tool that uploads the owner's own animated story videos to the owner's TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API. Videos are posted as private, and the owner reviews and publishes them in the TikTok app.

This tool is not offered to the public. It is used only by its owner on their own computer.

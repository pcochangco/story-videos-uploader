Privacy Policy

Story Videos Uploader (the "Tool") runs only on its owner's computer and is used only with the owner's TikTok account.

  • Data accessed: basic TikTok profile info (open ID, username, display name) and permission to upload and post videos, granted by the owner through TikTok Login.
  • Use: only to post the owner's own videos to the owner's account and to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Storage: access tokens are stored locally in the owner's macOS Keychain. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Deletion: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions), which invalidates the stored tokens.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/pcochangco/story-videos-uploader.
